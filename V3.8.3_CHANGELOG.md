# V3.8.3 — Mobile Stability Fix

- Fixed a rare mobile browser renderer crash while scrolling.
- Reduced full-screen canvas resolution and star density on mobile.
- Stops and removes the opening star canvas after entering.
- Pauses star animation while the page is hidden.
- Removed large filtered veil surfaces.
- Disabled blur-based reveal filters and scroll parallax on mobile.
- Separated moon float and scroll-depth transforms to eliminate motion conflict.
- Preserved all content and application features.
