# V3.10 — Gallery Experience

Built from V3.9.1.

## Enhancements
- Added velocity-aware swipe momentum with drag resistance and snap-back.
- Gallery navigation responds to either sufficient travel distance or a deliberate fast flick.
- Full-resolution loading is limited to the current and adjacent images.
- Thumbnail images load progressively as their buttons approach the visible filmstrip.
- Added a lightweight cinematic curtain opening when the gallery approaches the viewport.
- Added reduced-motion and mobile-safe fallbacks.

## Regression safeguards
- No moon markup or moon filters changed.
- No portrait styles changed.
- RSVP, guestbook, Maps, music, personalization and multilingual behavior remain unchanged.

## Functional files changed
- `index.html`
- `css/styles.css`
- `js/app.js`
