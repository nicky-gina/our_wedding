# V3.12 — Guestbook Enhancements

## Included
- Animated star arrival when a guest submits a new wish.
- Rebuilt accessible wish dialog with backdrop, focus management, Escape-to-close, and celestial styling.
- Search by guest name or message, including server-side filtered pagination.
- Random wish discovery from both the main guestbook and the open wish dialog.
- English, Indonesian, and Simplified Chinese translations for all new controls and states.
- Reduced-motion fallbacks.
